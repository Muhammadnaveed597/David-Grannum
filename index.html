I turn local stories into measurable footfall. I am the founder of IsleConnect and Vectis ONE, built on the Isle of Wight. IsleConnect is a visitor discovery platform. People scan a real place with their phone, unlock a short cinematic story, follow a themed trail, claim a local reward, and are guided towards nearby cafes, hotels, pubs, shops and attractions. The model is simple: Scan. Story. Reward. Footfall. Report. A business is not buying an advert. It is being placed inside a visitor journey, and we report what happened. Our first route, Queen Victoria and Imperial Ryde, is live as a prototype. Wartime Solent Defence is in development, and a Ryde smugglers route is in concept for hidden venues and the evening economy. Underneath sits Vectis ONE, the trusted AI-assisted production and governance layer: verified sources, human approval, cinematic media, QR journeys, local rewards, analytics and partner reporting. Before this, I spent over 30 years running fuel distribution businesses on the island, including more than a decade as Managing Director of Solent Fuels. I completed the Oxford Artificial Intelligence Programme at Said Business School in 2024. I am currently looking for: - Trail partners: Ryde venues, heritage sites and attractions - Civic and skills partners, including our youth AI and digital skills pilot - Aligned investors interested in place-based AI and trusted local intelligence If you run a venue, a heritage site or a local institution and want visitors you can measure, send me a message. Your first post, written for you
Ryde has a story problem. Not a shortage of stories. A shortage of ways to find them. The history is here. The venues are here. The visitors are walking past both. So we built the first IsleConnect prototype and put it live in Ryde: Queen Victoria and Imperial Ryde. It works like this. You scan a place. You get a two minute cinematic story. You follow the trail. You claim a reward at a local business. Simple to describe. Hard to make trustworthy. Five things the prototype taught us: 1. A story needs a source before it needs a script. 2. One verified story beats ten generated ones. 3. Local businesses are the destination, not the decoration. 4. A reward only matters if someone walks through the door to use it. 5. A report a business can actually read is worth more than a view count. That last point is the whole model. Scan, Story, Reward, Footfall, Report. The trail is live in Ryde now.
